Why I built this app
Deadline almost up.

I had to move an image I'd shot on my iPhone to my Mac and upload it, but the file wouldn't go through.

The photo was HEIC. The place I was uploading to said it didn't support that format.

So I found a converter site, uploaded the files one by one,
converted them, and downloaded them all over again.

That's when it hit me:

"Isn't there a tool that just does this in one go?"

So I built Dropfit myself.

Drag and drop, and you're done.

I think it'll help Mac users who, like me, can't stand hassle and busywork.

I'd really appreciate your interest.

Try it out, and leave any pain points or feedback in the comments. I'll improve it quickly.

I'm Jeonghoon Lee, the developer.
Thank you.
